Income Tax

Return filing, tax planning and representation for salaried individuals, professionals, firms and companies.

Income tax compliance has changed significantly with the Income-tax Act, 2025, which applies from 1 April 2026. I help individuals and businesses file accurately, plan within the law and respond to the department with confidence.

How I can help

  • Income tax return filing for individuals, HUFs, firms, LLPs, companies and trusts
  • Tax planning and regime selection based on your income profile
  • Advance tax computation and reminders
  • TDS / TCS returns, corrections and lower-deduction applications
  • Responding to notices, scrutiny assessments and rectifications
  • Appeals and representation before tax authorities
  • Capital gains computation on property, shares and mutual funds

Who this is for

  • Salaried employees and pensioners
  • Doctors, engineers, consultants and other professionals
  • Traders, shop owners and small businesses
  • Partnership firms, LLPs and companies

Documents usually needed

  • PAN, Aadhaar and bank account details
  • Form 16 / salary details and annual information statement
  • Bank statements, interest certificates and investment proofs
  • Books of account or business summary (for business income)
  • Purchase and sale documents for capital gains

Frequently asked questions

Should I choose the new or old tax regime?

It depends on your income, deductions and exemptions. I compare both for your actual figures before filing.

I received an income tax notice. What should I do?

Do not ignore it. Share the notice and your return details; most notices can be answered online once the reason is understood.
