Service

Accounting & Bookkeeping

Reliable books, MIS reports and payroll, so you always know where your business stands.

Good accounts are the foundation of every tax return, loan application and business decision. I set up and maintain books that are accurate, up to date and easy to understand.

How I can help

  • Bookkeeping in Tally, Zoho Books and other software
  • Bank and ledger reconciliations
  • Monthly MIS: profit, cash flow and receivables
  • Payroll processing, PF and ESI coordination
  • Preparation of financial statements
  • Clean-up of backlog accounts

Who this is for

  • Small and medium businesses
  • Startups without an in-house accountant
  • Professionals and clinics

Documents usually needed

  • Sales and purchase invoices
  • Bank statements
  • Expense bills and payment records
  • Employee details (for payroll)

Frequently asked questions

Can you work with my existing accounting software?

Yes. I work with Tally, Zoho Books and other common packages.

How often should books be updated?

Monthly at minimum, so GST returns, cash planning and tax estimates stay accurate.
