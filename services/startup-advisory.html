Startup Services & Advisory

Practical guidance for startups and growing businesses, from structure to funding readiness.

Early decisions about structure, registrations and finances shape a business for years. I help founders get these foundations right and prepare for growth and funding.

How I can help

  • Choosing the right business structure
  • Startup India (DPIIT) recognition
  • MSME (Udyam) registration
  • Project reports and loan proposals for banks
  • Financial projections and business plans
  • Basic funding readiness and compliance checks

Who this is for

  • First-time founders
  • Small businesses planning to expand
  • Businesses applying for bank finance

Documents usually needed

  • Business idea or plan summary
  • Founder KYC documents
  • Expected costs, pricing and sales estimates (for projections)

Frequently asked questions

What are the benefits of Startup India recognition?

Recognised startups can access certain tax, compliance and funding benefits, subject to eligibility conditions.

Can you prepare a project report for a bank loan?

Yes, including projected financial statements based on realistic assumptions about your business.
