Corporate Compliance

Company and LLP formation and ongoing compliance with the Registrar of Companies.

Companies and LLPs have regular filing and record-keeping obligations. Missing them leads to heavy late fees and penalties. I help you set up the right entity and stay compliant every year.

How I can help

  • Private limited company, OPC and LLP incorporation
  • Annual filings with the Registrar of Companies
  • Appointment and resignation of directors
  • Changes in share capital, registered office and objects
  • Maintenance of statutory registers and minutes
  • Conversion of partnership firms into LLPs or companies

Who this is for

  • Entrepreneurs starting a new business
  • Existing companies and LLPs
  • Partnership firms planning to convert

Documents usually needed

  • PAN, Aadhaar and photographs of directors or partners
  • Registered office address proof
  • Proposed names and business activities
  • Existing incorporation documents (for ongoing compliance)

Frequently asked questions

Should I start a company or an LLP?

It depends on funding plans, the number of owners and compliance appetite. I explain the trade-offs for your situation.

What happens if annual filings are missed?

Late fees accumulate per day of delay and directors may face disqualification. It is best to regularise as early as possible.
