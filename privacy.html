Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This website, casadique.com, is operated by Muhammad & Associates, Chartered Accountants, 3rd Floor, Grand Mall, Railway Station Road, Keloth, Payyanur, Kannur – 670 307, Kerala ("we", "us"). This policy explains what personal data we collect through the website and how we use it, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Information we collect

  • Enquiry form: your name, phone number, email address (optional), topic and message, when you choose to submit the contact form.
  • Direct contact: information you share when you email, call or message us on WhatsApp.
  • We do not use advertising trackers on this website.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and discuss the services you asked about.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your information for marketing purposes.

Service providers

To run this website we rely on: GitHub Pages (website hosting), FormSubmit (delivers enquiry form messages to our email), Zoho Mail (our email service), Google Fonts (typography) and YouTube (embedded videos, loaded in privacy-enhanced mode only when you press play). These providers process data only as needed to provide their services.

Retention

Enquiry information is kept only as long as needed to respond and for any professional engagement that follows, or as required by law and professional regulations.

Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct or erase personal data you have shared, or withdraw your consent, by writing to info@casadique.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Grievances

For any concern about how your data is handled, contact CA Sadique at info@casadique.com or +91 97445 70842.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.
